## Accessing the Guides and Reports
On the sidebar you will find a subsection named "Labs" and "Challenges". Each will have
a number corresponding to its laboratory exercise or challenge instance. For example,
Laboratory Exercise 01 is named *Lab 01* under the *Labs* subsection.
